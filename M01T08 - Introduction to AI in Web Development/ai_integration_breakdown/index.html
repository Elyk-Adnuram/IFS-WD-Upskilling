Prime Video

The platform I have chosen for this assignment is the Prime Video application from Amazon. It is a streaming platform that I have used in the past and it is similiar to other streaming platforms like Netflix.

Artificial Intelligence

Prime Video has a feature called X-Ray, which provides real-time information about the movie or series that a viewer is watching. This information includes details such as actor names, their career information and other interesting facts or trivia about the production. X-Ray analyses each video frame using computer vision to identify actors on screen, retrieve their career information from external databases like IMDb, and display relevant soundtrack details or trivia immediately.

Machine Learning

Prime Video uses Machine Learning (ML) algorithms to identify viewing patterns in its personalized recommendation engine. This is done by analyzing viewing history, search queries, ratings and watch duration of the viewer to determine which movie or series they would like to watch next. The ML algorithms use the mentioned factors to determine the next best video that the viewer might enjoy and provides this as a suggestion by dynamically updating the user interface in real-time.

Generative AI

Prime Video implements Generative AI (Gen AI) using a feature called X-Ray Recaps, that creates personalised text-based summaries of series and movies using large language models. This is a form of Gen AI because the X-Ray Recaps feature dynamically generates original text tailored to where a viewer is in a seasion and does not merely display pre-written summaries from another source
